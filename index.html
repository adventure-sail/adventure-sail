Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the most common questions before applying.

Do I need prior e-foiling or sailing experience?

No prior experience is necessary. E-foiling is significantly gentler on the body than surfing because the electric motor gives you continuous, smooth propulsion. Our certified coaches have complete beginners standing and flying comfortably within 90 minutes.

What does the 15-minute screening conversation involve?

Because our departures are small (5 to 8 guests maximum), we host a brief 15-minute conversation with every prospective guest before accepting a deposit. We use this to understand your expectations, answer questions about the boat, and pair compatible crews.

What happens if conditions get choppy or windy?

Mallorca offers dozens of protected, deepwater calas on different sides of the island. Our skippers choose sheltered anchorages with mirror-flat water for morning foil flights, and use the afternoon sea breezes for open-water sailing.

What about seasickness and comfort aboard?

Modern 50-foot cruising yachts are heavy, stable ocean vessels. We spend our nights anchored in calm, protected bays with zero ocean swell. For daytime sailing, we carry premium prevention remedies and keep coastal legs manageable.

What meals and drinks are included?

All-inclusive chef-prepared breakfasts, fresh Mediterranean lunches on anchor, snacks, and selected local Balearic wines are included. You can enjoy quiet dinners on deck under the stars or step ashore with the crew at coastal harbors.
