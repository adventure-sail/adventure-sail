# Strategy Meeting – Summary

**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Topic:** "10 questions for a strong marketing campaign" for a sailing-based adventure travel business

> Source: two raw transcripts in this folder. The transcription is rough (mixed Dutch/English, partly garbled), so some details are a best reading.

## What we're selling
- Adventure experiences built around a sailing boat. The boat is the anchor and where guests sleep. The activities are the draw.
- E-foiling is the starting activity. Other sports and adventures can be added later.
- A standardised package, not fully customised. Small tweaks possible if requested in advance.

## Positioning and price
- Definitely premium, but not for billionaires (they'd charter a big boat privately). Target is the upper-middle-class segment.
- Sits between a cheap sunset day tour (~50 people) and a private charter. Think ~5 people on a boat with champagne.
- Price ballpark:
  - ~€4,000 per person for a single cabin.
  - ~€7,000 per cabin when two people share.
  - All-inclusive except travel to the boat.
- Reference point: a friend running e-foiling trips reportedly charges ~€7,000/week. Shared cabins lower the per-person cost and keep the margin healthy.

## Target audience
- Main segment: adventurous people aged 40+ (roughly 35–60, some up to 65) who earn well. Solo, with friends or with a partner.
- Typical situation: kids are teens who no longer want family holidays. Surfing is hard on the body, and e-foiling is easier.
- Motivations:
  - Meet like-minded people and be part of a community.
  - Be looked after (e.g. food cooked for them).
  - Learn something new.
- Possible second wave: people in their 30s (e.g. padel players who don't want to start tennis).

## Problem we solve
- Doing this independently is a hassle: arranging activities, equipment, batteries and instructors.
- Starting from a boat means no crowded beach, it feels premium, and you can launch anywhere. On a beach you don't know the water depth.
- The offer is a "lifestyle package": everything organised and selected for you, with experts and a social element.
- Alternatives today: renting e-foils on a beach (e.g. Mallorca), day trips, or a standard resort holiday.

## Funnel and channels
- **Main:** Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, with YouTube for retargeting (up to ~3 months; legality to be checked).
- **Other:**
  - Windsurfing and e-foil communities and clubs.
  - Partner referral codes (e.g. 10% commission).
  - Influencers.
  - Brand-name Google search.
- **Geography:** still open. Germans are big in Mallorca and may prefer German-language channels.
- **Conversion idea:** a screening step instead of a hard sell. Collect contact details, learn what the person wants, then optimise conversion.
- Airbnb Experiences was considered but doesn't fit well, since this is a full holiday and not a few-hour activity.

## Central message (brainstorm, undecided)
- Themes: life isn't over at 40, freedom, a new chapter, still accessible and adventurous.
- Reference: BMW-style midlife advertising.
- Tagline idea: "New adventures on the horizon" (flagged as possibly too corporate).

## Decisions
- Start with e-foiling as the core activity.
- Offer a standardised, premium, all-inclusive package.
- Target adventurous 40+ customers, with Meta as the primary channel.

- Target geography and language.
- Call to action and screening flow.
- Final pricing.
