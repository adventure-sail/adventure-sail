# Campaign Brief – Five Key Points

**Source:** strategy meeting 2026-11-08 (see the transcripts and [summary.md](summary.md) in this folder)
**Status:** draft. Items marked *(open)* were not settled in the meeting.

## 1. Target: who are we trying to convince?

Adventurous, active people at a mature life stage who earn well and want to keep having good experiences.

> **Age rule:** we never state an age or age bracket in any public message, ad or landing page. Many people in the target would feel put off by being labelled. Age is shown through imagery, tone and life-stage cues only. *Internal reference for targeting and casting only:* core roughly 35–60, some up to 65.

- They travel solo, with a partner or with a few friends.
- Typical life stage: kids are teenagers who no longer want family holidays.
- They're sporty or outdoorsy (e-foiling, windsurfing, hiking) but find surfing hard on the body.
- They also want to sail. Sailing is part of the experience, not just transport, so people who enjoy being on a boat and learning to sail are part of the target.
- They like other active experiences too, such as hiking, for the days when they're not e-foiling.
- Premium, but not billionaire. They are upper-middle-class and would not charter a whole yacht.

## 2. Insight: what do they need, want or struggle with?

- They want an active, adventurous holiday but don't want a resort or a crowded beach.
- They want to be taken care of (food, logistics, a nice setting) without feeling like they've stopped being adventurous.
- They want to meet like-minded people. Doing this alone isn't very social.
- Organising it yourself is a hassle:
  - finding and booking equipment, instructors and spots;
  - batteries and charging (about an hour of riding per charge);
  - not knowing the water depth when launching from a beach.
- They have some money and feel their adventurous life isn't over. They want a new chapter, not a smaller one. *(Internal insight only. Never say this, or any age, to the customer.)*

## 3. Proposition: what are we promising them?

A premium, all-inclusive adventure holiday based on a sailing boat. Everything is organised for you, with e-foiling as the core activity, a like-minded small group, and good food and comfort.

- A standardised package, not a build-your-own trip.
- You sleep on the boat and launch from it, so you're away from the crowds, and the setting already feels premium.
- Pricing ballpark: about €4,000 per person (single cabin) or about €7,000 per cabin when two people share. All-inclusive except travel to the boat *(open: final pricing)*.
- Lower barrier to entry for both sailing and e-foiling than doing it on your own.

## 4. Reason to believe: why should they trust the promise?

- **Founders live the lifestyle.** They are real sailors and e-foilers, and so are the people they work with.
- **Industry knowledge.** They have a charter company background and know the market.
- **Experts on board.** Activities are run by experienced instructors, so guests don't have to figure things out themselves.
- **No proof assets.** We can't provide testimonials, guest photos or trip footage at launch. The campaign leans on the founders' credibility and expertise, and doesn't claim guest reviews or past trips we don't have.
- **Visuals:** use AI-generated and licensed stock visuals to illustrate the experience (atmosphere, boat, e-foiling, hiking). These are mood imagery, not proof. They are not presented as our own guests, trips or reviews. Check licences for anything found online.

## 5. Action: what do we want them to do next?

Not an instant booking. Start with a softer "get in touch" step.

- Leave their name, email and phone number through a short enquiry or screening form.
- We use it to learn what they're looking for and whether the trip fits them. If it doesn't, we say so.
- **Ask for preferences at sign-up.** For example: a women-only boat, a men-only boat, couples, friends, solo travellers, or other group makeups. We don't promise any option publicly. We collect the information first and decide later which options to offer, and whether they're standard or optional. Either way, we learn what demand looks like.
- We then follow up with details and guide them toward booking, and optimise the conversion rate from there.
- Retarget website visitors (e.g. Meta, YouTube) for up to ~3 months. *(open: check legality)*

## Positioning statement

> For **adventurous, active people** who **want an active, social holiday without the hassle of arranging it all themselves**, **Foil and Sail** offers **a premium, all-inclusive sailing-boat adventure with e-foiling, where everything is organised for you in a small like-minded group**, unlike **beach-based e-foil rentals, day trips, or generic resort holidays**, because **the founders are experienced sailors and e-foilers with a charter background, working with expert instructors, and we start from a boat that puts you away from the crowds**.

**Brand name:** Foil and Sail
**Tagline:** "New adventures await" ("New adventures on the horizon" was the alternative; the team prefers "await".)
**Destination:** Mallorca
**Languages / markets:** English and German (Germans are a big group in Mallorca). Campaigns, landing page and sign-up form run in both languages.

The "life isn't over at 40" angle is dropped from public use because of the age rule.
