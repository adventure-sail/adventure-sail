# Brand Key (Unilever-style) – Adventure Sail

**Source:** strategy meeting 2026-11-08, see [campaign-brief.md](campaign-brief.md) and [summary.md](summary.md)
**Note:** structure is the classic Unilever Brand Key (from memory, no template was available). Items marked *(inferred)* were not said in the meeting but follow from it. Items marked *(open)* are undecided.

## 1. Root strength: what do we bring to the table?

- Founders who live the lifestyle: real sailors and e-foilers.
- Charter-company background and knowledge of the market.
- Access to a boat as a base and to expert instructors.
- E-foiling can be done in almost any conditions, unlike surfing, which depends on waves.

## 2. Competitive environment: who are we up against?

- **Beach holidays:** renting e-foils with delivery and lessons (e.g. Mallorca), or doing it at home. Cheap, but you have to arrange everything and it's not very social.
- **Day trips and sunset sailing tours:** big groups (~50 people), not a real experience.
- **Private yacht charter:** for the very wealthy, and expensive.
- **Resort holidays:** comfortable, but not active, and crowded.
- **Other adventure holidays** such as kite-surfing trips. The sport is different, so only a partial alternative.

## 3. Target: whom are we talking to?

Adventurous, active people at a mature life stage who earn well. (We never state an age in public. Internal reference only: core roughly 35–60, some up to 65.) They travel solo, with a partner or with friends. Their teenage kids no longer want family holidays. They also want to sail, as part of the experience, and like other active add-ons such as hiking on the days they're not e-foiling.

## 4. Insight: what do they feel?

"I'm not done yet. I still want adventure, but my body can't take what it used to, and organising it all myself is a hassle. I'd also like to share it with people who feel the same."

- They want to stay active and keep having good experiences.
- They don't want a resort or a crowded beach.
- Planning, equipment, batteries and logistics put them off.
- Doing it alone isn't social.

## 5. Benefits

- **Functional:** everything organised and all-inclusive. You launch straight from the boat, with expert instructors, good food and no logistics.
- **Emotional:** feeling adventurous and alive, a sense of freedom and a new chapter.
- **Social:** meeting like-minded people in a small group and sharing the experience afterwards on the boat.

## 6. Values, beliefs and personality

*(inferred from the meeting)*

- **Values:** freedom, adventure, community, staying active, enjoying life.
- **Belief:** there are always new adventures to find, at any stage of life (internal only, never phrased around an age).
- **Personality:** adventurous but relaxed, premium without being snobbish, warm, sociable, confident. Think more "friends on a boat" than luxury yacht.

## 7. Reasons to believe

- Founders are experienced sailors and e-foilers with a charter background.
- Expert instructors on every trip.
- Starts from a boat, so you're away from the crowds and the setting already feels premium.
- Standardised package.
- No testimonials, guest photos or trip footage are available at launch, so we don't claim any. Visuals are AI or licensed stock, shown as mood imagery only.

## 8. Discriminator: what makes us different?

The only offer that combines a **sailing-boat base, e-foiling and a like-minded group in one organised, premium package**. Competitors give you the sport, the boat or the resort, but not all three, and not without hassle.

## 9. Brand essence

**Brand name:** Foil and Sail
**Tagline:** "New adventures await" (alternative: "New adventures on the horizon").
**Destination:** Mallorca. **Languages:** English and German.

The "life isn't over at 40" angle is dropped from public use because of the no-age rule.

Working suggestion *(inferred)*: **"Adventure, taken care of."** It captures the core promise of active adventure with no hassle and could serve as an internal essence line behind the tagline.
