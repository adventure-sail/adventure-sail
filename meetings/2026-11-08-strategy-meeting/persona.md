## Markus, the active explorer

*"I'm not ready to just lie on a sunbed. I want to do something good, with good people, and not spend a month planning it."*

### Snapshot

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Markus (fictional) |
| **Age (internal)** | 47 *(from meeting: core range roughly 35–60)* |
| **Home** | Munich, Germany *(assumed: Germans are a big group in Mallorca)* |
| **Languages** | German first, comfortable in English |
| **Work** | Senior role or runs his own business, earns well *(from meeting)* |
| **Family** | Partner; two teenage kids who'd rather stay home than go on a family holiday *(from meeting)* |
| **Travels** | Solo, with his partner, or with a couple of friends *(from meeting)* |
| **Budget** | Comfortable paying about €4,000–7,000 for a week, all-inclusive *(from meeting)* |

### Life and mindset

- Sporty and outdoorsy: windsurfs, hikes, probably skis. Surfing and other hard-on-the-body sports are getting harder *(from meeting)*.
- Feels his best adventures aren't behind him, but wants a version that fits his life now *(from meeting)*.
- Likes to be looked after: good food, nice setting, someone else organising *(from meeting)*.
- Enjoys learning something new, such as sailing or e-foiling, as part of the trip *(from meeting)*.
- Wants to meet people who share his outlook, not just be one more guest in a resort *(from meeting)*.

### Frustrations with how he holidays now

- Resort holidays are comfortable but passive and crowded.
- Doing an activity holiday himself means a lot of planning: equipment, instructors, spots, batteries *(from meeting)*.
- Beach e-foil rentals are fine, but it's a short slot, not social, and not a "trip" *(from meeting)*.
- Day trips and sunset tours are big groups and feel generic *(from meeting)*.
- Private yacht charter is too expensive and needs a crew of friends.

### What he wants from Foil and Sail

- Everything arranged: boat, e-foiling, sailing, hiking on the days between *(from meeting)*.
- A small, friendly group of like-minded people *(from meeting)*.
- A premium feel without a stuffy atmosphere. Launching from a boat, away from crowds, delivers that *(from meeting)*.
- To come home with stories, not logistics.

### How he feels on the trip

*(assumed, built from what he wants and what frustrates him)*

- **Free and unplugged.** Out on the water, away from crowds, work and the daily routine.
- **Looked after.** Nobody asks him to plan, book or fix anything. Meals, equipment and the day's activity are simply taken care of, so he can relax into it.
- **Alive and a little proud.** The rush of getting up on the foil for the first time, or trimming the sails, and the "I can still do this" feeling.
- **Part of something.** Evenings on the boat talking over the day with like-minded people, in a small friendly group instead of a crowd.
- **Curious and unhurried.** Learning something new without pressure, with a hike or a swim to fill the days between.

### How he feels after the trip

*(assumed)*

- **Recharged and lighter.** More energy and a clearer head than a sunbed holiday would leave him with.
- **Proud and confident.** "I tried something new and I was good at it." He feels more adventurous than before he left.
- **Connected.** He keeps in touch with some of the people he met and has a shared story with them.
- **Eager for more.** He talks about it to friends and family, shows photos and video, and starts thinking about the next trip, perhaps with his partner or friends.
- **Glad he did it.** The price feels justified because he came home with stories and a good feeling rather than just a break.

**The emotional journey in one line:** from *"I want to do something good but it's too much hassle"* to *free, looked after and alive on the trip* to *recharged, proud and already planning the next one.*

### Preferences he may state at sign-up

Group makeup is something we ask at enquiry and never promise publicly *(from meeting)*. Markus might say: "mixed group, couples welcome" or "I'd come with two friends." Other people in the audience might ask for women-only or men-only boats, or for solo travellers.

### How he decides

- **Finds us:** Instagram or Facebook (Meta ads), a German-language windsurf or e-foil community or club, a friend's recommendation or a partner's referral code, or a Google search for the brand name *(from meeting)*.
- **Needs to see:** the boat, the destination and the activities, plus who's running it. Since we have no guest reviews or past-trip footage at launch, he'll rely on the founders' sailing, e-foiling and charter background, so that has to be front and centre *(assumed)*.
- **Hesitations:** "Will I fit in with the group?", "Is it really worth the price?", "What if I'm not fit enough or not good enough?" *(assumed)*.
- **Won't respond to:** anything that labels him by age, or that sounds like a pitch. A low-pressure screening conversation suits him better *(from meeting)*.

### Messaging notes

- **Tagline:** "New adventures await."
- **Tone:** warm, confident, relaxed, premium but not snobbish.
- **Imagery:** relaxed, active people at a mature life stage on a boat, e-foiling at sunset, hiking in the hills. Never mention age, and show it through casting.
- **Language:** German and English versions. Don't just translate. Adapt for German tone.
- **Call to action:** a short enquiry form with preferences, and then a personal follow-up.

### Open questions to test

- Is he really the core buyer, or is it his partner who decides?
- Does he travel with a partner, friends, or solo, and which pushes him to book?
- How important is the sailing itself versus the e-foiling?
- Which preference options (women-only, men-only, couples) do people actually ask for?
- Which German-language channels and communities reach him best?

*Suggested next step:* run 5–10 short interviews with real people matching Markus (e.g. through windsurf and e-foil communities) and update this persona with what they say.
