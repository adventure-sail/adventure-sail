# Creative & Art Direction: Foil and Sail

## Visual Concept
The visual world of Foil and Sail bridges high-adrenaline watersports with refined Mediterranean sailing. It is bathed in natural Mediterranean sunlight, turquoise water, warm teak decking, and weathered limestone cliffs. The atmosphere is active, sophisticated, and effortless.

## Aesthetic Rules
- **No artificial glam or party yacht cliches:** No champagne sprays, strobe lights, or staged fashion shoots.
- **Authentic action:** Capture the exact moment of flight, water spray beneath the hydrofoil mast, and genuine smiles between sessions on deck.
- **Casting cues (Honoring the No-Age Rule):** Active, fit adults with sun-drenched athletic confidence. Their age is visible through maturity, posture, and poise, never through text labels or clichés.
- **Color Palette:** Deep navy blue (#1B365D), seafoam turquoise (#28B498), sunlit teak (#C68B59), and crisp sail white (#FFFFFF).

## Core Visual Prompts

### Visual 1: The Morning Flight
A fit, athletic adult gliding effortlessly on a sleek carbon-fiber e-foil over glass-calm turquoise waters in a sheltered Mallorca cove. In the soft-focus background, a modern 50-foot sailing yacht is anchored beneath dramatic Mediterranean limestone cliffs. Golden morning sunlight, crisp water droplets in motion, professional outdoor adventure photography.

### Visual 2: The Direct Transom Launch
Point-of-view perspective looking down from the teak swim platform of a sailing yacht onto an e-foil board floating in crystal-clear water. A hand in a sailing glove holds the wireless waterproof remote control. Turquoise seabed visible meters below, pure sense of freedom and readiness.

### Visual 3: Sunset Deck Gathering
A small group of 5 to 6 active adults relaxing around the cockpit table of a sailing yacht at golden hour. Mediterranean appetizers and wine glasses on the table, warm laughter, wet hair from a day in the sea, sun dipping below the Balearic horizon.
