## Email 2: The #1 Question (Do I Need Experience?)
* **Trigger:** 48 hours after Email 1.
* **Subject Line A:** "What if I have never e-foiled before?"
* **Subject Line B:** Learning to fly: what Day 1 on the boat looks like
* **Preview Text:** How our certified coaches have complete beginners gliding on day one.

### Body Copy
Hi [First Name],

The most common question we hear from guests is: *"Do I need prior e-foiling or sailing experience?"*

The short answer is no.

Unlike traditional surfing or kitesurfing, which demand upper-body endurance and precise wave timing, an e-foil is powered by a quiet electric motor controlled by a handheld bluetooth throttle.

Here is how our coaches guide you:
1. **Minute 1 to 20:** You start on your stomach, getting comfortable with throttle control and steering across the surface.
2. **Minute 20 to 45:** You rise to your knees, finding your center of balance as the board accelerates smoothly.
3. **Minute 45 to 90:** You stand up, lean slightly back, and experience the weightless magic of flying 50cm above the water.

Because we launch in calm, deep coves away from beach breakers, you learn in ideal conditions.
