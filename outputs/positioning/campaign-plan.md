# Campaign Plan: Foil and Sail

## Big idea

### Campaign Concept
Most holidays force a trade-off: either you sit passively in a resort chair, or you spend your vacation managing the exhausting logistics of renting gear, hauling batteries, and navigating crowded beaches. Foil and Sail solves both: you live aboard a beautiful sailing yacht in Mallorca, wake up in secluded turquoise calas, and step directly onto an e-foil in crystal-clear deep water with professional instructors handling every detail.

### Campaign Tagline
New adventures await.

*(Internal essence: Adventure, taken care of.)*

## Channel mix

| Channel | Funnel Role | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Ads (Instagram & Facebook)** | Top & Middle of Funnel (Awareness & Consideration) | E-foiling is mesmerizing on video. Gliding effortlessly above blue water alongside a sailing yacht creates instantaneous visual desire. Runs in English and German targeting outdoor and water-sports enthusiasts. |
| **Google Search Ads** | Bottom of Funnel (High Intent) | Captures searchers looking for "e-foil Mallorca", "luxury adventure sailing Spain", and "e-foil holiday Europe". |
| **Direct Community & Partner Outreach** | Middle of Funnel (Trust & Referral) | Partnerships with mainland and German e-foil clubs, windsurf associations, and premium outdoor gear hubs offering referral booking incentives. |
| **Email & Consultation Nurture (4-part)** | Bottom of Funnel (Screening to Booking) | Personalized email flow following the initial enquiry form to establish boat preferences (couples, solo, friend groups) and schedule a 15-minute founder call. |

## Budget split

Assuming a launch campaign budget of €10,000 [ASSUMED]:

* **Meta Ads Prospecting (50% / €5,000):** High-impact short-form video in DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and UK/expats.
* **Meta & Video Retargeting (20% / €2,000):** 60 to 90-day retargeting loop for landing page visitors with founder Q&A and route walkthroughs.
* **Google Search Ads (15% / €1,500):** Exact and phrase match keywords capturing high-intent adventure water-sports queries.
* **Email Onboarding & Screening Infrastructure (10% / €1,000):** Screening form tooling, CRM setup, and automated follow-ups.
* **Creative Testing Reserve (5% / €500):** Split-testing video hooks and German versus English ad copy.

## Risks and assumptions

1. **Strict Public Age Rule:** The campaign must strictly refrain from using age terms ("over 40", "middle age"). Tone, sophisticated aesthetics, high-end production, and mature models in visual prompts must carry the life-stage signal without alienation.
2. **Zero Proof Assets at Launch:** As a brand-new offering, no customer reviews or real trip footage exist. The campaign relies purely on founder background (charter and e-foil experience), clear equipment specifications, and transparent safety protocols.
3. **Screening Friction:** Asking for phone numbers and preferences up front creates friction. We must clearly explain that screening protects the small-group dynamic so every guest has a compatible crew.
