### Post 3: Proof Point
**Angle:** Proof Point  
**Calendar fit:** Day 5

The single biggest hesitation people have about e-foiling:

"What if I have never done board sports before?"

Traditional surfing takes months of paddling and upper-body fatigue just to catch a single broken wave. Kitesurfing requires managing lines, wind windows, and tricky beach launches.

An e-foil is completely different.

With an electric propeller and a handheld bluetooth throttle, our certified coaches have complete beginners standing and flying comfortably within 90 minutes.

Because our yacht anchors in protected, deepwater bays, you learn in flat water with an instructor right alongside you in the safety tender.

Small groups of 5 to 8 guests guarantee you get personal coaching every session.
