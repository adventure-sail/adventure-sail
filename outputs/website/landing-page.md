## Questions and Answers

**Do I need prior e-foiling or sailing experience?**
Not at all. E-foiling is significantly easier on the body than surfing or kiteboarding because the electric motor provides continuous, controllable propulsion. Most of our guests stand up and fly above the water during their very first session under our instructor's step-by-step guidance.

**How does charging and battery management work?**
Our yacht is custom-equipped with an onboard charging station and battery rotation system. While you ride, fresh packs are charging below deck. You never handle heavy chargers or worry about runtime.

**Can I travel solo or with my partner?**
Both. We offer individual single cabins as well as shared double cabins. Many guests join solo to meet other active outdoor enthusiasts, while couples enjoy having a private sanctuary on board with a vibrant social circle on deck.

**What is the screening process?**
Because we keep group sizes small (5 to 8 guests per voyage), we host a brief 15-minute conversation with every prospective guest before confirming a reservation. This ensures everyone on board shares similar expectations for activity levels, comfort, and group camaraderie.
