Foil and Sail

New adventures await.
Team: Adventure Sail | Destination: Mallorca | Target: English and German Markets

1. Campaign Positioning

Product: A premium, all-inclusive sailing yacht adventure holiday in Mallorca featuring e-foiling directly from the yacht's transom. Small groups of 5 to 8 like-minded guests with chef-prepared meals on anchor and expert instructors.

Audience / ICP: Active, adventurous professionals and couples who earn well and want high excitement without the pain of traditional surfing or the logistical nightmare of renting e-foils from crowded beaches.

The Core Insight: No Age Mention Rule Strictly Followed Guests want an active, thrilling new chapter without feeling like they have to settle for boring resorts or chaotic party flotillas.

The Single Promise: A premium, all-inclusive sailing adventure with e-foiling in Mallorca, completely organised for you in a small, like-minded group.

Proof Strategy: Founding team credibility (decades of professional Mediterranean charter experience and certified instructors), deepwater yacht launch safety, and zero artificial reviews or fake claims.

Single Conversion Goal: Submit an enquiry and screening form to request voyage itineraries and schedule a 15-minute consultation.
