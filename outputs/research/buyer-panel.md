# Buyer Panel Review: Foil and Sail Landing Page

## The Panel

1. **Mark, 49, Executive (The Burned One):** Paid €5,000 last summer for a "premium kitesurf camp" that was overrun with 22-year-olds drinking until 4 AM with broken gear. Extremely sensitive to operational chaos and misleading marketing.
2. **Claudia, 44, Senior Consultant (The Budget Holder):** Travel budget is healthy, but €4,000 for a single berth or €7,000 for a double cabin requires clear justification of what she is actually paying for compared to a 5-star hotel in Palma.
3. **Stefan, 52, Architect (The Loyalist):** Visits Mallorca every October, rents a private finca, and books 2 hours of e-foiling at a local beach club. Doubts that being confined to a boat adds real value.

---

## Panel Reactions

### Mark (The Burned One)
* **First Reaction:** "The boat and e-foiling combo looks incredible, but I have heard the 'like-minded small crew' pitch before and ended up stuck on a boat with people I could not stand."
* **Where I stopped believing:** "'Most of our guests stand up and fly above the water during their very first session under our instructor's step-by-step guidance.' What if conditions are choppy or the boat gets crowded?"
* **The one question I need answered:** "What happens if someone in the group is obnoxious, or the weather turns bad for two straight days?"
* **Would I take the action?** "Yes, but ONLY because of the screening call. If they actually screen out party animals and explain bad-weather alternatives, I would put down a deposit."

### Claudia (The Budget Holder)
* **First Reaction:** "At €4,000 for a single cabin, this is priced against luxury boutique hotels. I need to know the yacht specifications and whether I get private bathroom facilities."
* **Where I stopped believing:** "'Private cabins, chef-prepared meals on anchor.' The landing page does not state the yacht model, length, or whether cabins have en-suite heads (bathrooms)."
* **The one question I need answered:** "Is the cabin en-suite with air conditioning, or are 8 adults sharing two small heads down below?"
* **Would I take the action?** "Yes. Submitting an enquiry form costs nothing, and the screening step lets me interrogate them on cabin specs before committing funds."

### Stefan (The Loyalist)
* **First Reaction:** "Why would I sleep on a boat when I can rent a beautiful finca with a pool and just book an e-foil session at Port Adriano whenever I feel like it?"
* **Where I stopped believing:** "'You lug a 30kg board and battery across burning sand.' That is true, beach rentals are annoying with parking and lugging gear, but on land I have total control over my schedule."
* **The one question I need answered:** "How many hours of actual e-foiling do I get per day compared to the time spent sailing or waiting for battery charges?"
* **Would I take the action?** "No, not immediately. I need to see the daily itinerary breakdown showing riding time versus sailing time."

## Verdict Summary

* **Mark (The Burned One):** YES (convinced by the screening filter).
* **Claudia (The Budget Holder):** YES (form submission is low-risk, will ask about yacht specs).
* **Stefan (The Loyalist):** NO (wants a minute-by-minute schedule and cabin specs).

**Score: 2 out of 3 submit the enquiry.**

---

## Ranked Top 3 Objections & Recommended Fixes

### Objection 1: Lack of Yacht & Cabin Specifics
* **Quoted Line:** *"Settle into your private cabin, meet your skipper..."*
* **The Fix:** Explicitly state yacht specifications in the How It Works section: *"50+ foot modern cruising yacht with private double and twin cabins, dedicated heads, generator power, and spacious deck layout."*

### Objection 2: Daily Riding Time vs. Sailing Time
* **Quoted Line:** *"Spend the afternoon sailing to the next uninhabited cove... or relaxing on deck."*
* **The Fix:** Add an explicit session metric: *"Guests enjoy an average of 2 dedicated 45-minute e-foil flights per day with unlimited battery swaps, plus coaching feedback between sessions."*

### Objection 3: Bad Weather & Alternative Activities
* **Quoted Line:** *"Wake up in secluded turquoise calas..."*
* **The Fix:** Add a line on flexibility: *"Protected bay itineraries, scenic coastal hiking excursions, and Mallorca sheltered anchorages ensure continuous activity even if offshore swells pick up."*
