# Google Ads Search Campaign: Foil and Sail

## Campaign settings
* **Campaign Goal:** Leads (Screening form submissions)
* **Network:** Search Only (Google Search Partners disabled)
* **Suggested Geographies:** Germany, Switzerland, Austria, United Kingdom
* **Daily Budget:** €50 per day (split across both ad groups)
* **Bidding Strategy:** Maximize Clicks with CPC cap for the first 14 days, switching to Maximize Conversions once 15 leads are logged

---

## Ad group 1: E-Foil Experience & Holiday Intent
* **Intent:** Searchers looking specifically for e-foiling holidays, camps, or premium experiences.
* **Keywords (Phrase and Exact Match):**
