# Meta Ads: Foil and Sail

## Angle 1, Cold (Awareness & Pattern Interrupt)

### Concept
Interrupt the feed with the sheer magic of e-foiling over deep turquoise water, contrasting it with the misery of crowded beach rentals and resort loungers.

### Primary Text Variants
* **Short (114 characters):**
Glide above turquoise waters in Mallorca. No beach crowds, no heavy gear to carry. Your yacht is waiting.
* **Story:**
Most holidays make you choose between sitting by a crowded pool or lugging 30kg of water-sports gear across a packed beach.

We built a better way.

Foil and Sail is an all-inclusive sailing yacht adventure in Mallorca. You sleep on board, drop anchor in empty calas, and step directly onto an e-foil in calm, deep water with certified coaches.

Zero gear hassle. Zero crowded beaches. Just pure flight over open water.

### Headlines (under 40 characters)
1. Fly Above Water in Mallorca
2. E-Foiling Straight from a Yacht
3. No Beach Crowds. Just Flight.

### Link Card Description
All-inclusive sailing and e-foiling expeditions in Mallorca.

### Video Hook (0 to 3 seconds)
* **On-screen text:** Stop carrying batteries across crowded beaches.
* **Spoken line:** "What if you could launch an e-foil straight from your private yacht?"
* **Visual:** Close-up of an e-foil hydrofoil slicing cleanly through glass-calm turquoise water, pulling back to reveal a sleek sailing yacht anchored in a rugged limestone bay.

Curious about e-foiling, but tired of short 30-minute beach rentals with flat batteries?

On Foil and Sail, the yacht is your base. We rotate fully charged batteries all day while you explore Mallorca's wild coastline.

Every voyage includes certified instructors who have complete beginners standing and gliding on day one. Between sessions, enjoy freshly prepared Mediterranean meals on deck and sunset sails with a small crew of 6 to 8 guests.

### Headlines (under 40 characters)
1. 100% Coached. Zero Hassle.
2. The Yacht-Based E-Foil Trip
3. Private Cabins. Small Groups.

### Link Card Description
Explore Mallorca's hidden coves on a modern sailing yacht.

## Angle 3, Hot (Proof, Exclusivity & Screening Action)

### Concept
Target high-intent outdoor adventurers with small-group exclusivity (max 6 to 8 guests per yacht) and invite them to apply through the screening form.

### Primary Text Variants
* **Short (117 characters):**
Only 6 cabins per voyage. Explore Mallorca by sail and foil with a small, like-minded crew. Summer dates open.
* **Story:**
We run small-group voyages for active, outdoor people who value freedom on the water without the chaos of party flotillas or mass resorts.

Because our yachts accommodate only 5 to 8 guests, every prospective crew member completes a short screening conversation. This guarantees a compatible, motivated group on every departure.

Private single and double cabins available. All meals, yacht operations, coaching, and unlimited e-foiling included.

### Headlines (under 40 characters)
1. Limited to 8 Guests per Yacht
2. Reserve Your Mallorca Cabin
3. Small Crew. Big Adventure.

### Link Card Description
Request your voyage itinerary and screening consultation today.

### Video Hook (0 to 3 seconds)
* **On-screen text:** Only 6 to 8 guests per boat.
* **Spoken line:** "If you want real adventure in Mallorca without the crowds, look here."
* **Visual:** Sunset dinner on the cockpit table of the sailing yacht, guests laughing together with the sails furled and e-foils secured on deck.

## Audiences

### Cold Audiences
1. **Water-sports & Adventure Enthusiasts (UK, Germany, Austria, Switzerland):** Interests in Sailing, Foil surfing, Standup paddleboarding, Kitesurfing, Patagonia clothing, and Yacht chartering. Exclude: Cruising, All-inclusive resorts.
2. **High-Income Active Travelers:** Frequent international travelers interested in Mallorca, Balearic Islands, Trail running, Cycling, and Outdoor recreation.
3. **Broad Demographic Targeting:** Ages 35 to 60 (managed via ad set age filters, never in ad copy), household income top 25%, geotargeting Munich, Frankfurt, Zurich, London, and Hamburg.

### Warm & Hot Retargeting Pools
* **Website Visitors (30 to 90 days):** Exclude form completers. Serve Angle 2 and Angle 3 with video reviews of the yacht and route maps.
* **Meta Video Engagers (50%+ video watch time, 60 days):** Serve Angle 2 carousel highlighting onboard chef meals and coaching safety.
* **Lead Form Abandoners (14 days):** Direct message ad addressing single/double cabin availability and flexibility.

---

## Placements and test plan

* **Placements:** Instagram Reels, Facebook Feed, Instagram Stories (9:16 and 4:5 vertical video formats priority).
* **Test Plan:** Run Angle 1 (Cold) against Angle 2 (Warm) at €50/day split evenly across DACH and UK markets for 5 days. The winner will be judged on Cost Per Landing Page View and click-to-enquiry conversion rate.
