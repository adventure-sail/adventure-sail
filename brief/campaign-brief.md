# Campaign Brief: Foil and Sail

## Product

Foil and Sail is a premium, all-inclusive adventure holiday based on a sailing yacht in Mallorca. Guests sleep aboard, sail secluded bays, and experience e-foiling with expert instructors, launching directly from the boat away from beach crowds. Small like-minded groups (5 to 8 guests), freshly prepared meals, and zero logistical hassle.

## Audience / ICP

Adventurous, active individuals and couples who earn well and want genuine outdoor excitement without sacrificing comfort. They are outdoorsy (e-foiling, windsurfing, hiking, sailing) but find intense board sports like traditional surfing hard on the joints. Life stage cues: teenagers who no longer join family holidays, looking for an active new chapter. They travel solo, as couples, or with a few friends.

*Internal targeting note only:* Core age 35 to 60. Under NO circumstances do we mention an age or age bracket publicly.

## Problem they have

Organising an e-foiling or active ocean holiday independently is exhausting: sourcing rental boards, charging batteries (only 60 minutes of ride time per pack), navigating shallow beach launches, and dealing with crowded tourist beaches. Meanwhile, standard resort holidays are boring and passive, party flotillas are chaotic, and private yacht charters cost a fortune.

## Promise

A premium, all-inclusive sailing adventure with e-foiling in Mallorca, completely organised for you in a small, like-minded group.

## Proof

- Founded by lifelong sailors and active e-foilers with an established charter company background.
- Expert certified instructors and experienced skippers on board every voyage to handle all training, gear, and safety.
- Direct-from-yacht deepwater launch: no carrying 30kg batteries across crowded sands or hitting hidden rocks.
- High-spec ocean sailing yachts equipped with charging stations, premium boards, safety tenders, and private cabins.
- Strictly honest marketing: we do not use fake testimonials or stock photos dressed up as past guests. Founding team credibility and transparent trip design lead every message.

## Conversion goal

Submit an enquiry and screening form (name, email, phone, and travel preferences) on https://foilandsail.com/enquiry to request trip dates and a screening consultation.

## Tone

Adventurous, confident, warm, premium.
- Sounds like: Patagonia, boutique expedition lodges, high-end sailing clubs.
- Never sounds like: Sandals, Royal Caribbean, The Yacht Week, cheap discount tours.

## Do not say

- Absolute ban on stating age, age brackets, or terms like "over 40", "middle age", "midlife", or "retirees".
- Do not use: "booze cruise", "cheap getaway", "luxury pampering", "couch potato", "seamless", "unlock".

## Second audience

German-speaking professionals and active couples (Germans represent the largest premium adventure travel demographic in Mallorca). Campaigns and pages run in English and German.

## Competitors

- Beach-based e-foil rental outfits in Mallorca (hassle with batteries, short rentals, no social or accommodation element)
- Day charter catamarans (50+ tourists, loud music, crowded bays)
- High-end resort hotels in Mallorca (comfortable, but passive and physically uninspiring)
